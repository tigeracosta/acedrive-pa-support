Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is covered in AceDrive PA?

A: The app covers 100% of the material in the official Pennsylvania Driver's Manual, organized into gamified skill nodes and an 18-question DMV practice exam simulator.

Q: How do I manage my account and data?

A: You can review your progress, manage your account, or request data deletion directly inside the app on your Profile settings screen.
